# DIGITAL IRIS — acceptance tests

Status values: **PASS**, **PARTIAL**, **FAIL**, **UNVERIFIED**.

"Console" means a screenshot from DuckStation booted from `DIGITAL_IRIS.CUE`
with `-slowboot`, i.e. through the BIOS CD boot path. "Preview" means the host
software model of the GPU, which is a development aid and is never on its own
treated as proof that something runs on a PlayStation.

Console evidence: `reports/emulator/ps1_00.png` … `ps1_13.png`, captured in
one continuous run, and `ps1_timeline_montage.png`.
Preview evidence: `reports/frames/`.

## Hard gates

| Gate | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **G0** Isolation | **PASS** | The repository was empty at the start (`git log` had no commits). Nothing outside this project was touched. The supplied baseline is preserved byte-for-byte in `baseline/` and is never an output path. |
| **G1** PS1 native | **PASS** | `dist/DIRIS.EXE` is a PS-X EXE, load and entry `0x80010000`, body 77824 B. `dist/DIGITAL_IRIS.BIN` is ISO9660 in raw Mode 2 Form 1. 15/15 structural checks pass (`reports/disc_verify.json`). No browser, no video, no pre-rendered frames. |
| **G2** CD boot | **PASS** | Booted with `-slowboot`, so the BIOS reads `SYSTEM.CNF`, resolves `cdrom:\DIRIS.EXE;1` and loads it. Direct EXE loading was not used anywhere. The baseline's boot-name mismatch is fixed and now verified: `boot_matches_iso_directory: true`. |
| **G3** One world | **PASS** | Fuji, the lake, the bank and the plant are in one camera and one light. The orbit test (T15) shows them parallax against each other. |
| **G4** Procedural on the console | **PASS** | All geometry and both textures are generated by the ROM at boot or per frame. The ISO contains only `SYSTEM.CNF` and the executable — there is nothing else on the disc to load. |
| **G5** Reproducible | **PASS** | `make clean && make ps1 iso` from source; toolchain versions pinned in BUILD.md; SHA256 in `reports/SHA256SUMS.txt`. |
| **G6** Honest performance | **PASS** | Measured 15–20 fps and reported as such, including the fact that it misses the 25–30 fps target. Method and raw readings in `reports/PERFORMANCE.md`. |

## T01–T18

| # | Test | Status | Evidence and notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| T01 | Seed on textured 3D soil | **PASS** | Console `ps1_00.png`: the seed is a lit ellipsoid resting in soil whose cracked-earth texture is visible to the bottom of frame. The texture is a cellular (Worley) field synthesised on the console. |
| T02 | Germination adds geometry | **PASS** | Console `ps1_01.png`→`ps1_02.png`: the shoot is new ribbon geometry emerging from the splitting seed, not the seed scaled up. The seed's two halves part as the shoot rises. |
| T03 | Continuous stalk and leaf development | **PASS** | Console `ps1_02.png`→`ps1_03.png`. Leaves are ribbon surfaces integrated from an elevation angle that varies along their own length; they lengthen and arch continuously. |
| T04 | Bud has volume under camera change | **PASS** | Preview `orbit_-45/0/45.png` at the bud stage; the bud is three sheath bracts plus six closed tepals, and it occludes itself as the camera moves. |
| T05 | Six functionally different tepals, curvature, veins | **PASS** | Console `ps1_04.png`, `ps1_05.png`. Three falls swing out and hang; three standards stay upright and arch inward. Veins come from the petal texture, a parametric vein field in the ribbon's own u/v, so they follow the surface as it bends. Yellow beard present on each fall. |
| T06 | Camera rise, dolly-out, parallax | **PASS** | Console `ps1_00.png`→`ps1_07.png`: one continuous move from near the soil to the wide composition. The bank, lake and mountain separate at different rates. |
| T07 | Mt Fuji is a real solid that divides light and shade | **PASS** | 10 rings x 18 segments, a genuine cone with a ragged snowline. Orbit test shows it move against the plant. At sunset (`ps1_05.png`) the lit and shadowed faces differ. |
| T08 | Grass: ≥2 LODs, clustering, out-of-phase wind | **PASS** | Three LODs (ribbon blades / one triangle per blade / one triangle per clump), placement by a masked jittered lattice, and a per-clump wind phase offset so no two tufts swing together. Ablation: `frames/ablate_no_grass.png`. |
| T09 | Lake: perspective, ripple, sunset and moon glitter | **PASS** | Console `ps1_05.png` (sunset sheen) and `ps1_07.png` (moon path). The glitter is driven by the wave slope, not painted on. |
| T10 | Sunset changes surface appearance, not just the sky | **PASS** | Console `ps1_05.png`: the mountain's faces, the petals and the grass all change tone and direction of shading, not only the sky gradient. |
| T11 | Moonlit frame in which iris and Fuji stay readable | **PASS** | Console `ps1_07.png`: dark frame, moon upper right, mountain and flower both legible. |
| T12 | Fireflies: trajectory, pulse, depth, local light | **PASS** | Console `ps1_06.png`, `ps1_07.png`. Deterministic drift paths, asymmetric blink, depth-sorted against the grass, each with one dim additive rectangle on the ground as a local-light approximation. |
| T13 | Determinism | **PASS** | Same seed twice → byte-identical frames at t=15/58/96 s; a different seed → different frames at all three. Verified on the preview build, which runs the same generators as the ROM. |
| T14 | Ablation changes only its own aspect | **PASS** | Measured pixel deltas against the same frame: grass 810 px, texture 6944, fireflies 90, water 25779, iris 2236. Each switch changes a bounded region and nothing else. Images in `frames/ablate_*.png`. |
| T15 | ±45° orbit does not reveal a billboard | **PASS** | `frames/orbit_-45.png`, `orbit_0.png`, `orbit_45.png`. Different petals face the camera at each angle, the flower occludes itself, and Fuji moves independently. |
| T16 | Freeze geometry, rotate the light | **PARTIAL** | START freezes the sun, and the light direction is keyframed independently of geometry (`frames/ablate_freeze_sun.png` holds the opening light at t=96 s). A full 120° sweep over frozen geometry is not exposed as a single control, so this is not demonstrated as one continuous test. |
| T17 | LOW / BALANCED / SHOWCASE differ without breaking | **PARTIAL** | The three profiles differ in primitive load — at t=96 s: 586/713/829 primitives, 1016/1155/1249 triangles — and none breaks the scene. Frame rates per profile were **not** measured on the console; only BALANCED was. |
| T18 | ≥3 continuous runs, no overflow or degradation | **PARTIAL** | Multiple continuous console runs of 2–4 minutes each with no packet or OT overflow (`overflow = 0` on every frame of the preview sweep) and no visible degradation. Full 120 s cycles were watched on the console but not three complete back-to-back cycles under observation, so this is reported as partial rather than claimed. |

### Negative tests

| Check | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Near-plane crossing | **PASS** | Polygons approaching the GTE's divider saturation point (H ≥ 2·SZ, i.e. nearer than 140 units at H=280) are transformed on the CPU, clipped against z = near, projected by hand and then clipped to the screen rectangle. Without this the near ground tore across the frame. |
| GPU primitive size limit | **PASS** | Anything whose projected extent approaches 1023x511 is routed through the same CPU path and trimmed, so no primitive is silently dropped by the GPU. `oversized = 0` on every measured frame. |
| Primitive arena exhaustion | **PASS** | `render_alloc` returns NULL and increments a counter rather than writing past the end. Never triggered in any measured frame. |
| Extreme seeds | **PASS** | Seeds 0x1715 and 0x2BE7 both produce valid scenes (T13). |
| Missing controller | **PASS** | The SIO0 poll is bounded and backs off to once every 64 frames when the port does not answer. Unbounded waits here cost more than the entire renderer in the first console build. |

## Known gaps

- **No real hardware.** Nothing here was run on a PlayStation. All console
  evidence is DuckStation.
- **No PCSX-Redux cross-check.** Only one emulator was used.
- **No audio.** The SPU is untouched; the piece is silent. See ISSUES.md.
- **15–20 fps, not 25–30.** Measured and reported, not worked around.
